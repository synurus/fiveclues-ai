What to write about

Bug reports

If a game won't start, a correct answer was marked wrong, or the page looks broken, please let us know. Including the following helps us find the cause much faster:

  • The date and approximate time it happened
  • Your device and browser (for example, iPhone Safari or Windows Chrome)
  • The word, what you typed, and any message on the screen

Words and clues

Suggestions like “this word is too obscure”, “these two can't be told apart from clues” or “please add this word” help us refine the word list. If a specific clue was wrong or strange, the 👎 and comment on the result screen work too — the whole game record is saved with it, which makes the cause easy to find.

Deleting feedback

To remove feedback you left, email us with the GitHub issue link or number, or the date, nickname and word. We'll delete it once we've found it. See the Privacy Policy for more.

Anything else

Collaboration ideas and questions about how the game was made are welcome too.

Response time

Since this is a one-person project, a reply may take a few days, but every message is read. Common questions about the rules are answered in How to play.
