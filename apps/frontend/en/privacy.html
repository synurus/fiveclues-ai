Privacy Policy

Five Clues (다섯고개, “the Service”) handles only the minimum information needed to run the game. This policy explains what the Service processes, why, and how. It is an English translation of the Korean Privacy Policy; if the two differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Basic principles

  • The Service has no sign-up or login. It never asks for your name, phone number, email address or other identity information.
  • The Service runs no database of user information. The state of a game in progress is encrypted and kept in your browser; the server only verifies it.
  • Only feedback that you choose to send is recorded in a public repository (section 2).

4. Advertising and cookies

The Service may show ads through Google AdSense. The Service itself doesn't set cookies, but Google and its partners that serve ads do.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, and opt out of other third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized ads at www.aboutads.info.
  • See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings. Blocking cookies doesn't stop you from playing.

5. Deletion

Guesses you type are used only to check answers and generate clues, and are not stored on the server. Feedback in the public repository is deleted promptly when you ask. Feedback stays on record even after it has been used and closed, so please ask separately if you want it removed.

6. Children

The Service doesn't collect identity information and doesn't check ages. Children should not put personal information in the feedback nickname or comment. If a parent or guardian finds feedback containing a child's information, email us and it will be removed right away.

7. Your rights and how to make a request

You can ask to see, correct or delete feedback you left. Because there are no accounts, please include whatever you know of the following so we can find it:

  • The GitHub issue link or number
  • The date and approximate time, nickname and word of the game

Send requests to synurus@gmail.com. We'll handle them as soon as we confirm them and reply with the result.

8. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • The game in progress (including the secret word) is encrypted with AES-256-GCM before it's handed to your browser, so neither you nor anyone else can read or alter it.
  • Because the Service collects no identity information and keeps no database, there's very little personal information that could leak in the first place.
